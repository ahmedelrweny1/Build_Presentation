The 5 Golden Rules of Student AI

Use AI as your tutor and springboard—never as a substitute for your brain.

1. Never Copy Blindly

Always rephrase in your own genuine voice and check flow.

2. Always Verify Facts

Check primary sources on Perplexity or Google Scholar manually.

3. Learn, Don't Replace

Use AI to explain concepts so you actually pass your in-person exams.

4. Respect Privacy

Never paste confidential personal data or unreleased university exams.
